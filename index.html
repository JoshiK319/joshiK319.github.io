Research focus

Ultra-High-Speed Motor Drives

Delay-aware MIMO control · TI C2000 · 100,000 rpm design speed

At high speed, PWM delays can destabilize a drive. I design delay-aware multivariable (MIMO) control for ultra-high-speed PMSMs and carry it from simulation to experimental setup.

95,000 rpm on hardware · 188% larger speed margin

Real Time HIL Validation

PLECS RT Box · Speedgoat · Typhoon HIL · Speed control · PFC

A controller that works in simulation still has to work with real timing and real hardware. I validate speed-control designs and power factor correction (PFC) control in hardware- and software-in-the-loop on PLECS RT Box FPGA models. At Cummins I also integrated a TI LaunchPad with Speedgoat and Typhoon real-time simulators.

Cummins: compressor drive to 60,000 rpm at full torque

High-Speed Motor Control and PFC

Direct Torque Control · 200 kW XM3 inverter · ePLL-based PFC

I have built a Direct Torque Control drive with a smooth open-loop to closed-loop start-up, using a 200 kW XM3 inverter and a fuel-cell compressor motor. I have also developed ePLL-based power factor correction control for a 400 V DC output, validated in HIL.

DTC drive validated to 60,000 rpm

Grid and Renewable Integration

PV-STATCOM · wind–solar hybrids · Newton–Raphson load flow

Inverters and feeders can do more than they usually do. I have worked on PV inverters that also supply reactive power (PV-STATCOM), modeled wind–solar hybrid systems for off-grid communities, and written Newton–Raphson load-flow tools to estimate distribution losses.

Loss study across 20 NEA substations
